# NiskBuild — Product Overview (Marketing Summary)

**Describe your app. Get real code. Keep it forever.**

NiskBuild is the AI app builder that doesn't lock you in. Type what you want in plain English, watch AI build it in real time, and walk away with actual source code — not a rented app trapped on someone else's platform.

## The problem with most AI builders

Type a prompt into most AI app builders, and what you get back lives on their platform, under their terms. Stop paying, and it's gone. Your work, your ideas, your business — held hostage by a subscription.

## How NiskBuild is different

Every app you build on NiskBuild comes with clean, exportable code you can open, edit, host anywhere, or hand to a developer. If you ever leave NiskBuild, your apps don't leave with us.

**Build fast with AI. Leave whenever you want, with everything you made.**

- **Describe, don't code.** A plain-English prompt is enough to generate a working app — a business site, a booking page, a dashboard, whatever you need.

- **Take it anywhere.** Export clean source code, deploy a live preview instantly, or package it for mobile.
- **Try before you commit.** Start free, no card required.
